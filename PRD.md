## 1. Product Overview

**Name:** EGS AI (repo name "EGA-AI"). Official Release Version (Production Launch).

**What it is:** An Arabic-language AI tutor ("smart assistant / teacher") for students of the **Egyptian national curriculum** — middle school (اعدادي) and high school (ثانوي). Students chat with an AI that answers strictly from their grade's uploaded curriculum, take AI-generated exams, get AI grading with feedback, and upload images of problems for analysis.

**Subscriptions & Pricing:**
- **Monthly Pro Subscription (`pro_1m`):** 60 EGP / 30 days, 80 points per day. Full access to Deep Thinking (Reasoning Mode), daily point renewals, and exam generation.
- **Two-Month Subscription (`pro_2m`):** 110 EGP / 60 days, 90 points per day.
- **Three-Month Subscription (`pro_3m`):** 140 EGP / 90 days, 120 points per day (hero tier).
- **Point deduction (Fair Metering, section 9.1):** every plan deducts at the same real-cost rate; plans differ only in daily points, so the 3-month plan always buys the most usage. A per-term fair-use pace guarantees no plan can cost more than its revenue.
- **Cancellation & Refund Policy:** Requests allowed within 3 days (72 hours) of purchase strictly provided that the user has consumed 0 points from the subscription package.
- **Payment Processing:** **Kashier (كاشير)** payment gateway on Web (Visa, Mastercard, Meeza, Vodafone Cash, Orange Cash, Etisalat Cash, WE Pay, Instapay; SSL 256-bit PCI-DSS encrypted).
- **Support Contact Channels:** Phone / WhatsApp: `01037220587`, Email: `business@egsaiedu.com`, dedicated Web page `/contact`.

**Platforms:**
- **Web:** Next.js single-page app at `web/` + Progressive Web App (PWA) with offline Service Worker support and desktop/mobile installation — Arabic, RTL, dark/light themes. Production domain: `https://egsaiedu.com`.
- **Curriculum Generator:** Standalone Next.js application at `Curriculum Generator/` running on localhost (port 3005) for concurrent multi-curriculum PDF single-pass multimodal extraction (one request per page reads, classifies and organizes it) & RAG Markdown optimization powered by DeepSeek 4.1 (`deepseek-flash`). Every book is first reduced to its official outline (read from the table-of-contents page, or typed by the operator), and all unit/chapter/lesson headings are aligned to that outline, so the lesson map students see matches the printed book.

**Target users:**
- **Students** (role `student`): each student belongs to one stage (صف دراسي) of an education system (نظام تعليمي), optionally one track (مسار/شعبة) of that stage and one elective subject. Systems, stages and tracks are managed by the admin (section 4, Education Catalog). Seeded catalog: **التعليم العام** = `1_middle`, `2_middle`, `3_middle`; **البكالوريا المصرية** = `1_high`, `2_high` (4 tracks: الطب وعلوم الحياة، الهندسة وعلوم الحاسب، إدارة الأعمال، الآداب والفنون). Added 2026-09-28 (inactive until the admin enables them): **الثانوية العامة** = `2_secondary_general` (tracks علمي `sci_general_secondary` / أدبي `lit_general_secondary`) and **التعليم الأزهري** = `2_secondary_azhar` (علمي `sci_azhar_secondary` / أدبي `lit_azhar_secondary`). New accounts start at `unselected` and pick their path in onboarding.
- **Admin** (role `admin`): manages education systems, stages, tracks, curricula (including shared and elective subjects), placeholder subjects, users, notifications, reports via the web admin dashboard.
- **Guests / Visitors:** Unregistered visitors can browse curriculums and view introductory materials, but all pedagogical AI chat, image analysis, and exam generation strictly require registration to prevent misuse of expensive model quotas. Free trial points (20 points) are granted only upon verified registration.

**Business model (planned):** point ("coins") economy metered per token consumption. Plans `free` / `pro` / `max` exist in data + UI badges, but **payments are disabled during beta** (UI promises payments "before August 2026"). Deep Thinking (Reasoning Mode) is strictly gated to active paid subscribers and administrators; free users receive high-speed direct responses without thinking mode.

**Key product rules:**
- The AI answers from the student's textbook, including questions that need reasoning from it (applications, "why / what if", everyday phenomena), and never labels those as outside the curriculum. A scope remark appears only for a genuinely beyond-curriculum academic point: one natural, topic-specific sentence written by the model (no fixed template, no "تنبيه:" label) telling the student it is not required for their exam. General-knowledge, study-advice, casual and non-academic messages never get a curriculum disclaimer (section 8.2.1).
- All user-facing text is Arabic (Egyptian-friendly tone). Math in LaTeX. Diagrams as sanitized inline SVG.
- The chatbot persona is an intellectually poised, smart, sophisticated, and polite Egyptian educational mentor.

## 4. Core Domain Concepts

- **Education Catalog (الأنظمة والمراحل والمسارات التعليمية)** — `web/src/lib/education_catalog.ts` (pure, client-safe) is the single source of truth for study paths. It is stored as JSON in `system_settings.education_catalog` (no schema migration) and served to clients by `/api/config` (`education_catalog`):
  - `systems[]` `{id, name, description, order, active}` — e.g. التعليم العام، البكالوريا المصرية، الثانوية العامة، التعليم الأزهري، a future American diploma.
  - `stages[]` `{id, systemId, name, level: primary|preparatory|secondary|other, order, active, electivesEnabled}` — the stage id is what `profiles.grade_level`, `curriculums.grade_level`, `chat_sessions.grade_level`, exams and progress rows store, so legacy ids (`1_middle` … `2_high`) keep working unchanged.
  - `tracks[]` `{id, stageId, name, description, order, active}` — stored in `profiles.track_id` / `curriculums.track_id`.
  - `subjectLinks{curriculumId: {stageIds[], trackIds[]}}` — **shared subjects**: one uploaded curriculum can also appear in other stages or systems (e.g. the 2nd-secondary physics book is uploaded once under `2_secondary_general` and shared into `2_secondary_azhar`) and be opened to extra tracks, without duplicating chunks or embeddings.
  - **Visibility rule** (`isCurriculumInPath` / `curriculaForPath`): a curriculum is in a student's path when the stage is its own `grade_level` or listed in its links; when it is restricted to tracks of that stage (`track_id` + linked tracks), the student's track must be one of them; elective curricula (`is_elective`) in a stage with `electivesEnabled` show only the elective the student chose (`profiles.elective_subject`, one per student; defaults to the first option). Employs resilient elective matching (`isElectiveMatch`, `getBaseSubjectName`, and `normalizeSubjectBase`) that normalizes Arabic characters and strips volume/part/semester suffixes (e.g. `- جزئ اول`, `- جزء 1`, `(الفصل الأول)`), ensuring that students with base discipline names (e.g. `فيزياء`, `رياضيات`) or part-specific choices (e.g. `فيزياء - جزئ اول`) match renamed or multi-part curricula seamlessly.
  - **Resolution** (`resolveCurriculum`, used on both client in `page.tsx` and server in `curriculum_structure.ts` and `db.ts`): `(stage, subject)` maps to the stage's own curriculum first, then to one shared into the stage through `subjectLinks`. Features fuzzy Arabic normalization (`normalizeSubjectMatch`), base discipline extraction (`getBaseSubjectName`), and fuzzy subject discipline matching (`isSubjectFuzzyMatch`), stripping the definite article 'ال', tatweel, diacritics, and unifying alef/taa-marbuta/yaa variants (e.g. matching 'فيزياء', 'الفيزياء', and 'فيزياء - جزئ اول'; 'رياضيات', 'الرياضيات', and 'رياضيات - جزئ اول'; 'التاريخ المصري' and 'التاريخ  - جزئ اول'), plus stage alias matching across sister stages of the same academic level (such as `2_secondary_general`, `2_secondary_azhar`, and general secondary `stage_munoffuv0uy`). Client-side checks (`isSessionCourseValid`, `hasCurriculum`, message verification, tasks units) use `resolveCurriculum` directly, ensuring shared curricula appear instantly available and interactive in the chat without false 'unavailable' warnings.
  - **Validation:** `validateStudentPath` (update-grade, complete-onboarding), `sanitizeInitialPath` (register, Google), `sanitizeCatalog` (admin saves; ids `^[a-z0-9][a-z0-9_]{1,39}$`, reference checks, limits). Deactivating a stage/track hides it from new selections but a student can keep the one they already have. Deleting a stage/track with students or curricula is refused (409).
  - **Legacy settings:** on first load (no stored catalog) the default catalog is seeded from `active_grade_levels` / `active_tracks`; afterwards both lists are derived from the catalog (`legacyActiveLists`) and written back on every save for older clients.
  - Stage names and levels are registered server- and client-side through `registerStageCatalog` (`subject_helpers.ts`), so `getGradeDisplayName`, the chat style profiles (`getChatStyleProfile`) and exam specifications (`specGrade`) handle admin-created stages by their level.
- **Placeholder Curricula (المناهج قيد الإعداد والتجهيز):** Admin can register upcoming subjects without immediately uploading Markdown content (`is_placeholder = true`). Placeholder subjects display normally to students with a "قيد الإعداد" badge and a friendly modal notice, while chat messaging, exam generation, and flashcards are temporarily disabled until the admin attaches the curriculum file.
- **Subjects:** free-form Arabic text (e.g. الأحياء, الكيمياء, الرياضيات المتخصصة). A subject exists for a stage when an admin added/uploaded a curriculum for `(grade_level, subject_name)` or shared one into that stage.
- **Curriculum:** one Markdown document per (stage, subject) in the canonical format of section 8.4, chunked hierarchically (parent sections + embedded child chunks) for RAG. Admin publishes/unpublishes via `active_curriculum_ids`; stages and tracks are enabled in the Education Catalog.
- **Coins:** usage currency. Registered users receive 15.0 non-renewable trial points upon verified registration (`free_trial_grants`), plus a daily freemium lifeline (5.0 coins) replenished daily up to their plan cap (free 5.0 / pro_1m 80 / pro_2m 90 / pro_3m 120 — `DAILY_COIN_CAPS`); unauthenticated visitors have 0 coins and must register to use AI services. Deducted per token usage (see section 9). `unlimited_credit` flag or `admin` role bypasses deduction.
- **Plans:** `free` / `pro` / `max` on `profiles.plan_type` — determine the daily coin cap (section 9); otherwise cosmetic during beta (badges; Deep Thinking unlocked for subscribers).
- **Session:** a chat conversation (`chat_sessions`), tied to a subject + grade; messages in `chat_history`.
- **Unified AI Model:** Single high-performance model across the platform: `deepseek-flash` (DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash with native multimodal vision, ultra-low latency, and high mathematical accuracy). The differentiator between tiers is **Deep Thinking (Reasoning Mode)**: enabled exclusively for active subscribers and admins, disabled for free users.
- **Interaction modes:** `socratic` / `detailed` (default) / `summary` — per-session AI teaching style (`chat_sessions.mode`), selected in the chat composer and appended as a behavioral block to the system prompt.
- **Reports:** students can flag an AI answer; admin reviews (`pending`/`reviewed`/`dismissed`).
- **Notifications:** general platform broadcasts (`user_id NULL`) or targeted personal notifications (e.g. subscriber activation confirmations with `user_id UUID`), with types `info`/`success`/`warning`/`maintenance` targeted to `web`/`phone`/`both`; dismissals stored client-side.

### 6.5 `curriculum_chunks` (RAG v2, hierarchical)
| Column | Type | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| id | UUID | PK |
| curriculum_id | UUID | FK → curriculums, CASCADE delete |
| content | TEXT | NOT NULL |
| heading | TEXT | NOT NULL (special heading `__CURRICULUM_SUMMARY__` at position_index −1 holds the Gemini-generated summary; special heading `__RAW_MARKDOWN__` at position_index −99 stores the exact original Markdown document for 100% fidelity without requiring external schema alterations) |
| chunk_level | TEXT | `'parent'` (full section) or `'child'` (sliding-window sub-chunk) — default `'parent'` |
| parent_id | UUID | self-FK, CASCADE; set on children only |
| position_index | INTEGER | NOT NULL default 0 |
| embedding | VECTOR(768) | children only (text-embedding-004) |
| fts_arabic | TSVECTOR | trigger-maintained, `'simple'` config |
| fts_english | TSVECTOR | trigger-maintained, `'english'` config |

Indexes (since 2026-10-05): partial btree `idx_chunks_child_embedded (curriculum_id) WHERE chunk_level='child' AND embedding IS NOT NULL`, so vector search is an exact cosine scan over one curriculum's children (at most ~1.4k rows, about 6 ms warm) with perfect recall. The 101 MB HNSW index was dropped because every query is curriculum-scoped: the planner rarely used it, it competed for the small shared buffer cache, and every embedding write paid HNSW maintenance. GIN on `fts_arabic` (the unused `fts_english` GIN was dropped); btree on `parent_id` and `(curriculum_id, chunk_level)`; partial `idx_chunks_special (curriculum_id, heading) WHERE position_index < 0` for the `__CURRICULUM_SUMMARY__` / `__RAW_MARKDOWN__` lookups (those queries add `position_index < 0`).

Trigger: `curriculum_chunks_update_fts` (BEFORE INSERT/UPDATE) recomputes both tsvectors from `heading || ' ' || content`.

RPC function: `hybrid_search_curriculum(p_curriculum_id, p_query_embedding VECTOR(768), p_arabic_query TEXT, p_english_query TEXT, p_match_count INT DEFAULT 8, p_rrf_k INT DEFAULT 60)` — Reciprocal Rank Fusion over (vector cosine, Arabic BM25, English BM25), each top-50, child chunks only, returns top N with `rrf_score`. The chat retriever calls it with empty text queries, i.e. as a pure HNSW vector search (`db.vectorSearchChunks`); lexical search runs in memory (section 8.3).

**Parent-link defect (found and repaired 2026-09-30):** uploads before this date linked every child to the FIRST parent carrying the same breadcrumb heading, so children of repeated sections (examples, exercises, split parts) pointed at an unrelated section: 4,350 of about 18,000 production children (24%) were mislinked, and 374 parents had no child at all, so dense search could not reach them (266 in `1_middle` العلوم). The chunker now links by parent index (`curriculum_processor.ts` and the bulk `upload_*.mjs` scripts). The retrieval corpus re-links children in memory at load time, so chat is correct even before the data is repaired; `node web/scripts/repair_chunk_links.mjs` (dry run by default, `--apply` to write) fixes the stored links and creates embedded children for orphan parents; it was applied to production on 2026-09-30 (4,350 children relinked, 374 orphan sections given embedded children) and a follow-up dry run reported no remaining issues.

### 6.21 Performance layer and chat media storage (`supabase_migration_performance.sql`, 2026-10-05)
- **Why:** user messages carried full base64 photos (up to 4 MB each): `chat_history` was 119 MB for 2,881 rows, every chat turn re-read the whole session with its photos, and the 224 MB shared buffer cache was thrashed, so cold vector searches took 0.6-1.5 s.
- **Chat media:** private Storage bucket `chat-media` (15 MB per object, image and audio MIME whitelist, service role only). `/api/chat` uploads the photo or voice note (`offloadInlineMedia`, path `<user_id>/<yyyy-mm>/<uuid>.<ext>`) before saving the row and keeps the original payload if the upload fails; `/api/chat/history` and `/api/admin/users/conversations` replace `media:` references with 6-hour signed URLs in one batched call (`resolveMediaRefs`); `page.tsx` `mediaSrc()` renders signed URLs and legacy base64 alike; voice bubbles use `preload='none'`. `db.deleteUser` removes the user's storage folder. Existing rows were moved with `web/scripts/offload_chat_media.mjs` (upload, read back and size-check, then rewrite the row).
- **Indexes added:** `chat_history (user_id, sender, created_at)` and `(session_id, created_at)`; `chat_sessions (user_id, created_at DESC)`; `flashcard_decks (user_id, created_at DESC)`; `exam_submissions (user_id, submitted_at DESC)`; partial `user_devices (user_id, last_active_at DESC) WHERE is_active`; FK indexes on `contact_messages.user_id`, `exams.session_id`, `reports.user_id`, `reports.session_id`; the curriculum chunk indexes of 6.5.
- **Indexes dropped (duplicates or never used; each slowed writes):** `payment_transactions_order_id_idx`, `idx_voice_usage_user_date`, `idx_user_devices_user_id`, `idx_user_devices_token`, `chat_sessions_engagement_idx`, `idx_profiles_welcome_claimed`, `idx_profiles_apology`, `idx_profiles_improve_ai_opt_in`, `profiles_coins_idx`, `notifications_active_idx`, `idx_curriculums_placeholder`, `idx_curriculums_track`, `idx_curriculum_entities_category`, `idx_curriculum_relations_type`, plus the HNSW and English GIN indexes.
- **RPCs:** `get_leaderboard` is one aggregate pass (LEFT JOIN of per-user exam averages) instead of a correlated `AVG` per student; `get_user_leaderboard_rank` reuses it. `admin_user_activity()` returns per-student message count, active Cairo days, exams created/completed, decks and cards for the admin directory (it replaced shipping every chat row to the server, which PostgREST also capped at 1,000 rows, so counts were wrong). All have `search_path = public`.
- **Hardening:** EXECUTE on server-only RPCs revoked from `PUBLIC`/`anon`/`authenticated`; `search_path` pinned on the trigger and search functions; `study_notebook` RLS policies use `(SELECT auth.uid())` (evaluated once per statement).
- **App-side query changes:** `getChatHistory(..., limit)` selects explicit columns and reads only the newest N rows (chat turn 60, history API 100); `findUserAiMessage` replaces loading a user's entire history in the notebook resolver; `validateUserSessionDevice` selects three columns; `updateDeviceActivity` writes `last_active_at` at most once per 5 minutes per device (in-memory throttle); `/api/admin/users` loads devices for all listed users in one query (`getActiveDevicesByUser`) instead of one query per user, and pages profiles past 1,000 rows; `/api/config` passes the loaded profile to `getDailyQuests` (one profile read instead of two).

### 6.20 Local-dev fallback
`web/src/lib/db.ts` uses `./db_data.json` for local Node.js development and an in-memory database store (`globalThis.__egs_memory_db`) whenever the filesystem is read-only (`NO_DISK_DB`: Edge runtime or any Vercel deployment, detected via `VERCEL`), both when Supabase env vars are absent and when a Supabase fallback path reads the local store. No disk scan or write happens at module load on Vercel. It mirrors the tables above and seeds default admin and test accounts. On deployment, **Supabase is mandatory** (`NEXT_PUBLIC_SUPABASE_URL` and `SUPABASE_SERVICE_ROLE_KEY` in environment).

## 7. Backend API Reference

All routes under `web/src/app/api/`. Conventions:
- **Bearer auth:** `Authorization: Bearer <token>` — custom HMAC token (section 10).
- **Guest header:** `x-device-id` (client-generated, spoofable).
- **Admin:** Bearer + DB check `profile.role === 'admin'` (an `authorizeAdmin()` helper duplicated per admin route).
- All routes run on the Node.js runtime (the default); none declare `runtime = 'edge'`. Edge isolates cold-started per route and dominated Active CPU usage, and Next.js 16.3 drops Edge support for routes. Heavy routes also set `maxDuration = 60`.
- **Sliding-window rate limiting** protects authentication and intensive endpoints (`rate_limiter.ts`), decoupled into account (per-email) and network (per-IP) limits to support classroom NATs and concurrent traffic. Error messages are Arabic.

## 8. AI Pipeline (RAG v5: multi-query hybrid retrieval, cross-encoder reranking, scope-aware answering)

- **DeepSeek** `https://api.deepseek.com/v1/chat/completions` — `deepseek-flash` (unified single model: DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash with native multimodal capabilities and configurable thinking mode). Powers real-time chat streaming, student tutor persona, multimodal problem image analysis, exam generation, grading, flashcards, query intelligence (`analyzeQueryIntelligence`), HyDE passage generation, curriculum summaries (`generateCurriculumSummary`), student engagement classification (`classifyEngagement`), and and the answer-scope classification that decides how a reply is framed. Reasoning Mode (Deep Thinking) is enabled exclusively for active subscribers and admins, while free users receive fast direct answers with thinking disabled.
- **Curriculum Generator (Localhost:3005 Multimodal Engine):** **DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash (`deepseek-flash`)** reads each page image (plus the PDF text layer), classifies the page and writes organized Markdown in one request per page; the legacy EdenAI/Gemini OCR path and `eden_vision.ts` were removed. Pipeline (section 8.6): single-pass page extraction (default 8 pages in parallel) with the outline discovered during the run, then deterministic assembly and heading alignment.
- **Alibaba Cloud DashScope** `https://dashscope-intl.aliyuncs.com/compatible-mode/v1/embeddings` — `text-embedding-v4` (primary embedding model with Matryoshka Representation Learning configured for 768 dimensions, directly populating Supabase pgvector `VECTOR(768)` with zero database migrations; batch size $\le 10$). Query and chunk vectors MUST come from this same model; `web/scripts/reembed_curricula.mjs` re-embeds every child chunk (`heading\ncontent`) and must be run whenever the model changes (it was run on 2026-09-25 after the bulk upload scripts had embedded most curricula with EdenAI `text-embedding-004`, which made vector search return unrelated sections).
- **Alibaba Cloud DashScope Rerank** `https://dashscope-intl.aliyuncs.com/api/v1/services/rerank/text-rerank/text-rerank` — `qwen3-rerank` (`rerankDocuments` in `gemini.ts`, 6 s timeout, instruction: retrieve passages holding the facts or principles needed to answer, including by reasoning). Scores the top 20 fused sections against the standalone question (about 0.4-0.6 s). Measured on production curricula (2026-09-30): in-curriculum questions score 0.65-0.90, genuinely beyond-curriculum ones 0.40-0.56.
- **Alibaba Cloud DashScope Speech-to-Text (ASR):** Powered by **Qwen3-ASR-Flash (`qwen3-asr-flash`)** via `https://dashscope-intl.aliyuncs.com/api/v1/services/aigc/multimodal-generation/generation` (fallback `qwen-audio-3.0-asr-flash-filetrans`). Ultra-low-latency synchronous speech recognition (<1s) with native Arabic phonetic alignment, backed by an Egyptian Dialect & Curriculum Phonetic Refinement layer (`refineEgyptianTranscript`) that preserves 100% colloquial Egyptian while repairing phonetic mishearings of science/English terms.
- **EdenAI** `https://api.edenai.run/v2/text/chat` (Gemini 2.5 Flash as multi-provider fallback for query intelligence, gap checks, summaries, and exam generation), `/v2/text/embeddings` (Google text-embedding-004, 768-dim as fallback dense vector embedding), `/v2/image/question_answer` (VQA google→openai as fallback for student image analysis), `/v2/audio/speech_to_text_async` (STT google→openai, Arabic, polled).
- **Token Efficiency & Meta-Prompting Strategy:** System prompts, metadata extraction schemas, and structural formatting rules are written in dense, unambiguous English instructions to reduce prompt token consumption by ~40-50% and enhance reasoning adherence. DeepSeek performs Arabic curriculum analysis and outputs Arabic HyDE passages and keywords, while operating under a strict high-priority directive mandating friendly, authentic Egyptian colloquial Arabic (`اللهجة المصرية التعليمية المهذبة`) for all student explanations.
- **Resend** for OTP email.

### 8.2 Tutor prompts (`tutor_prompts.ts`, scenario-specific since 2026-09-30)
The single ~41-47K-character prompt (every rule for every grade, subject and mode, with the retrieved textbook text in its middle) was replaced by prompts assembled per scenario. A chat request is sent as:
1. **System message** `buildTutorSystemPrompt({subjectName, gradeLevel, mode})` (about 9.3-9.9K characters): it depends only on the session's grade, subject and teaching mode, so it is byte-identical for every student in the same scenario and DeepSeek serves it from the prompt cache (cache hits cost 1/50 of a miss). Blocks, most stable first so different scenarios still share the cached prefix:
   - `CORE` (shared by all): persona and tone (refined Egyptian educational Arabic, substance-first openings, no flattery for asking, no filler honorifics, praise only for earned achievements), no emojis, language purity, KaTeX-only math with no raw Unicode superscripts or × ÷, image and voice notes as first-class input (never claims to be text-only), no unrequested lesson roadmaps; USING THE TEXTBOOK (excerpts are the authority, solved-example method, never invent book details, follow-ups); REASONING (silent 5-step checklist: what is asked, governing textbook facts, explicit inference steps, gap filling consistent with the book, verification); CURRICULUM SCOPE (section 8.2.1).
   - `SESSION`: subject, grade (Arabic and English), curriculum name.
   - `STAGE_PROMPTS`: **preparatory** (young student, plain words then the book's definition, lesson depth only, everyday Egyptian examples, mark-earning model answers) or **secondary** (mature, mechanisms and conditions, learning outcomes and higher-order thinking). Other stage levels use the preparatory block; admin-created secondary stages use the secondary block.
   - `GRADE_PROFILES`: per-grade vocabulary, depth and word budget (`1_middle` 120-300 words, `2_middle` 150-350, `3_middle` 200-400, `1_high` 250-500, `2_high` as the reasoning requires with the Baccalaureate zero-rote rules). Admin-created stages borrow the `1_high` or `2_middle` profile under their own name.
   - Subject method by family (`getSubjectFamily`): **science** (definition, reason, experiment; المعطيات / القانون / التعويض / الناتج; Latin LTR notation with Western digits exactly as the textbook; SVG diagram rules), **math** (problem method; Arabic RTL notation with Eastern digits, ق / ل / ح_ن / جا, braced exponents and full expansions, or Latin notation for English-track math via `determineMathNotationContext`; Latin variables kept when the student's own problem uses them; SVG rules), **Arabic** (rule then full إعراب, rhetoric and its effect, reading), **English** (all English content and every question 100% English, bold vocabulary, listening passages), **humanities** (causes, results, significance with the book's exact dates; source criticism, motives, counterfactuals; geography factors; philosophy arguments), **general**.
   - `MODE_PROMPTS[mode][stage]`: **detailed**, **socratic** (preparatory: one or two simple guiding questions, a strong hint after two tries, the step after a third; secondary: sharp questions on givens, principle and next step, targeted hint after two attempts), **summary** (quick review / exam review), **step_by_step** (four labelled problem-solving stages).
   - Boundary redirection texts for this subject and grade (`buildBoundaryRedirectMessage`, section 8.2.2), question rules (Modern Standard Arabic or 100% English, comprehension over recall, plausible distractors, randomized correct position, strict card gating), and card formats with one example quiz card for the subject family.
2. **Earlier turns** (up to 14 messages, as stored), so the system message plus history stays a cacheable prefix across a conversation.
3. **Final user message** `buildTurnMessage(studentMessage, context, lessonMode)`: `<textbook>` (the `[SCOPE CHECK]` note, lesson map and excerpts, or the capability/greeting directions of the fast lane), then `<lesson_workflow>` only in lesson modes (`FULL_LESSON_WORKFLOW`: whole lesson in one message with headings, exam angles, summary, one quiz card and a next-lesson question, no `[LESSON_NAV]`; `STEP_LESSON_WORKFLOW`: the roadmap phrase "درسنا اليوم هو ...، والدرس ده مقسم إلى [N] نقاط أساسية" that the route detects, one point per message, re-explaining on "مش فاهم", and the exact `[LESSON_NAV]` JSON), then `<student_message>`.

`deepseek.ts` now only builds the messages (`buildChatMessages`) and calls the API (`generateChatResponseStream(turn)`, `staticChatCompletionStream`). Measured on 2026-09-30: input per ordinary reply fell from about 12K+ tokens, nearly all billed as cache misses, to about 2.5-5K tokens of which the system prompt (about 2.2K tokens) is a cache hit after the first request of a scenario; answers, full lessons, step-by-step lessons with `[LESSON_NAV]` and Socratic guidance were verified end to end.

#### 8.2.1 Curriculum scope and natural framing
Six scopes, chosen per message by `resolveAnswerScope` (section 8.3) and passed as the `[SCOPE CHECK]` note (`buildScopeHint`), which is authoritative: *curriculum* and *reasoning from the curriculum* (applications, "why / what if", everyday phenomena, multi-lesson and exam inference questions) are taught as curriculum content, naming the lesson and deriving inferred answers step by step, never with a scope remark; a wrongly cited lesson is corrected in one sentence; *beyond the curriculum* gets a correct ~150-word answer that starts with the substance and mentions scope exactly once, in one natural topic-specific sentence near the end saying it is not required for the exam (a lesson is linked only when that adds a concrete insight; no quiz cards); *general knowledge / curiosity / study or exam advice* gets a helpful answer with no disclaimer; *casual* a brief warm reply; *non-academic* one or two friendly sentences steering back. Fixed disclaimer templates, the "تنبيه:" label, unexplained "helps you understand the lesson" claims, repeated scope wording and invented placements are forbidden. The chat route also drops a leading "outside the curriculum" line from any reply whose scope is not beyond-curriculum (`isScopeDisclaimerLine`; the first line is held until a newline or 280 characters).

#### 8.2.2 Subject and grade boundaries
The redirect is served without calling the model when the classifier sets `isDifferentGrade`, or when it sets `isDifferentSubject` AND `isClearlyDifferentSubject(active, detectedSubject)` (`SUBJECT_FAMILIES` in `subject_helpers.ts`: humanities, sciences, math, Arabic, English, other languages, religion, computing; unknown labels, the active subject, integrated branches and sibling disciplines never qualify) AND none of the question's first three keywords appears in the textbook (`curriculumTextContains`). The fixed texts come from `buildBoundaryRedirectMessage(subject, grade, 'grade'|'subject'|'both', english)` and are streamed through `staticChatCompletionStream` (zero tokens, zero points), in English when the subject or message is English:
  * Grade: `"يبدو أنك في [اسم الصف الدراسي]... للإجابة بناءً على منهجك المحدد، يرجى الانتقال إلى 'الملف الشخصي' (Profile) وتحديث صفك الدراسي."`
  * Subject: `"المنهج المتاح لدي حالياً هو [اسم المنهج]... للإجابة بناءً على منهجك، يرجى بدء محادثة جديدة واختيار المادة المطلوبة. (كما نذكرك بالتأكد من اختيار صفك الدراسي الصحيح من خلال 'الملف الشخصي' (Profile))."`
The system prompt quotes the same texts as a fallback; redirections carry no cards, and greetings, platform questions and study advice never trigger them. Interactive tags: `[QUIZ_QUESTION]`, `[CREATE_EXAM]`, `[CREATE_FLASHCARDS]`, `[LESSON_NAV]`, `[LISTEN_PASSAGE]` (section 11).

### 8.3 RAG flow (live `search_step` SSE events)
0. **Conversational & Multimodal Capability Fast-Lane Check:** greetings and introductions (`isConversationalGreeting`) and questions about sending images or voice notes (`isImageCapabilityInquiry`, `isVoiceCapabilityInquiry`) skip retrieval and stream an immediate reply with no curriculum disclaimer.
0.5. **Exhaustive Full Lesson Retrieval Mode (`getExhaustiveLessonContext`):** If the student requests a lesson explanation (`isLessonRequest`):
    - Identifies the matching lesson via `findMatchingLessonInCurriculum`: lesson/unit numbers (`الدرس 1`, `الدرس الأول`, colloquial `التاني`/`التالت`, `الفصل`/`الوحدة`) first, then overlap of *significant* title words (structural words and ordinals are ignored). No match means it is not a lesson request and the turn falls back to normal retrieval.
    - Extracts the lesson text with `extractLessonBlocks` (every matching `## الدرس` block of the raw markdown, capped at 30,000 chars); `success` requires a matched lesson with at least 300 chars of textbook text. The curriculum lesson map is prepended.
    - `isSinglePointQuestion` marks concept/law/term/problem questions as single-point even when a lesson is named.
    - Sets `lessonMode = 'full_lesson'` (or `'step_by_step'` for turn-by-turn guidance); the answer scope is curriculum.
1. **Query intelligence** `analyzeQueryIntelligence(query, subject, grade, lessonMap, conversation)` (`gemini.ts`; DeepSeek Flash, thinking disabled, JSON mode, 900 max tokens; Gemini via EdenAI as fallback). The prompt puts the static instructions, subject and lesson map (titles plus up to 8 subtopics each) first so the provider prompt cache is reused, then the last 4 turns and the message. Output: `{scope, queryType, isLessonExplanation, lessonTitle, curriculumLesson, relatedLesson, isDifferentSubject, detectedSubject, isDifferentGrade, detectedGrade, standaloneQuery, searchQueries, arabicKeywords, englishKeywords, hydePassage, searchAnnouncement}`.
   - `scope` is one of `curriculum`, `curriculum_reasoning`, `beyond_curriculum`, `general_education`, `casual`, `non_academic` (definitions in `QueryScope`). A lesson placement turns a beyond verdict into `curriculum_reasoning`.
   - `standaloneQuery` restates follow-ups ("طب ليه؟", "حل مسألة زيها") as one self-contained textbook question; `searchQueries` are 1-3 textbook-phrased sub-queries that also cover the laws or mechanisms an inference question needs (e.g. "why does a hose spray faster when partly blocked" -> continuity equation, flow rate). `curriculumLesson` / `relatedLesson` are validated against the map.
   - The in-memory corpus load starts in parallel with this call (`getCurriculumCorpus`).
   - Subject/grade mismatch routing is unchanged (section 8.2.2).
2. **Casual and non-academic messages** skip retrieval: the context is the lesson map plus a `[SCOPE CHECK]` note.
3. **Retrieval** `retrieveCurriculumContext` (`hybrid_retriever.ts`):
   - **Corpus** (`getCurriculumCorpus` in `db.ts`): every parent section and child chunk of the curriculum, a BM25 index and the knowledge graph (all entities and relations, paginated), loaded once per curriculum and cached in memory (12 curricula, 30-minute TTL, cleared by `invalidateCurriculumsCache`). At load, children whose parent does not contain their text are re-linked to the parent that does (section 6.5), and parents without children are indexed directly.
   - **Dense:** one batched `text-embedding-v4` call embeds the HyDE passage, the standalone question and the sub-queries (up to 4 texts); each vector runs a 30-result HNSW search (`db.vectorSearchChunks`).
   - **Lexical:** Okapi BM25 over the whole book (`bm25.ts`): Arabic normalization, Eastern digits to Western, dialect and function-word stop list, Light10 stemming (conjunction `و`, `ال`-family prefixes, common suffixes), real term frequencies, a lightly weighted breadcrumb, and a bigram proximity bonus for multi-word terms. Searched with the keyword string and each query phrasing. (The previous version prefiltered 250 chunks with Postgres FTS, computed IDF over that subset only and counted every term once per chunk.)
   - **Graph:** `KnowledgeGraphEngine.linkEntities` links an entity only when its full name occurs in the question or keywords, or all of its name stems do (single common words no longer link), keeps the 12 most important, and contributes the parent sections of the activated sub-graph. The synthesized graph text is no longer injected into the prompt.
   - **Fusion:** weighted RRF (k = 60) at the section level: every child hit counts for its parent, once per list at its best rank (dense 1.0 / 0.9, keyword BM25 1.0, query BM25 0.8, graph 0.5).
   - **Reranking:** the top 20 sections are scored by `qwen3-rerank` against the standalone question; order = relevance plus a 0.03 fusion tie-breaker. Kept: at least 3 sections, then those within 0.22 of the best score and above 0.30, at most 8. Without reranking the first 7 fused sections are kept.
   - **Expansion:** for the top 3 sections, adjacent parts of a split section ("heading (1)", "heading (2)") are added in book order. Total budget 18,000 characters.
   - **Context:** lesson map + "مقتطفات الكتاب المدرسي ذات الصلة بالسؤال" with each excerpt's breadcrumb. With no chunks at all, the raw Markdown is split by headings and ranked with the same BM25 (`markdownFallback`).
   - Overview questions also get the curriculum summary and outline.
4. **Answer scope** `resolveAnswerScope` (`gemini.ts`): casual / non-academic as classified; curriculum and reasoning -> curriculum; general -> general (curriculum if a lesson was placed); beyond -> accepted only when the core term (`arabicKeywords[0]`) is absent from the textbook text (`curriculumTextContains`) AND the best rerank score is below `STRONG_TEXTBOOK_RELEVANCE` (0.72), otherwise curriculum; an unclassified question is never treated as beyond. For general and beyond answers the excerpts are cut to at most 2 with relevance >= 0.6 so loosely related text does not invite forced links. `buildScopeHint` then prepends the `[SCOPE CHECK]` note.
5. **Search steps:** `analyzing`, `searching`, `graph_traversal`, `fusion`, `found` (plus `summary`, `lesson_search`, `lesson_ready`, `checking`), using the existing icons the web client maps to Lucide.
6. **Streaming:** DeepSeek streams `reasoning_content` -> `thought` and `content` -> `content` events; usage is captured for billing.
7. **Evaluation (2026-09-30, production data, 28 questions over 2nd-secondary physics, 1st-prep science and 1st-secondary history):** scope 28/28 correct (curriculum and inference questions never flagged; relativity, black holes, DNA replication and World War I identified as beyond; study-plan questions general; thanks casual; football and cake recipes non-academic); the correct lesson was in the top 3 sections for every retrieval question; classifier about 1-2.5 s and retrieval about 1.2-1.8 s once the corpus is warm (about 4.6 s on the first question of a curriculum). Generated answers contained no "تنبيه:" template, and beyond-curriculum answers put a single natural scope sentence at the end.

### 8.4 Curriculum ingestion pipeline (`curriculum_processor.ts`)
Unified for initial upload (`/api/admin/curriculum`) and detail edits (`/api/admin/curriculum/detail`):
Source Markdown is stored in `curriculums.content` → split into **parents** by heading with a full breadcrumb: a `#` heading directly followed by another `#` heading is a parent unit (group), so paths read `الوحدة > الفصل > الدرس > section`; the `# منهج …` title line and HTML comments are ignored; the solution/hint/variation parts of a worked example (`الحل`, `الإجابة`, `وسيلة مساعدة`, `ماذا لو`, `فكرة الحل`, `خطوات الحل`) stay inside the example's section so a question and its answer are retrieved together (PARENT_MAX 500 tokens) → **children** via sentence-aware sliding window (CHILD_MAX 120 tokens, 24-token overlap, Arabic `؟` aware), each linked to its own parent by index (`ChildChunk.parentIndex`) → embeddings (`text-embedding-v4`, 768-dim; the admin UI and upload scripts embed pending children progressively) → curriculum summary `__CURRICULUM_SUMMARY__` (position −1; a structural fallback is used when no LLM is reachable) → **Knowledge Graph** via `buildKnowledgeGraph(parents, curriculumId)`: entities with the same normalized name are merged, and relations are re-pointed to the surviving entity, deduplicated and stripped of self-loops.

**Graph relation fix (2026-09-28):** relations used to keep the ids of merged duplicate entities, so almost every 50-row insert into `curriculum_relations` failed its foreign key and production held **0 relations** for nearly every curriculum (GraphRAG traversal returned nothing). All curricula were rebuilt from their stored parent chunks (about 8,000 relations restored), except `3_middle` الرياضيات and `2_middle` العلوم, which already held more relations from an older extractor and were left unchanged.

**Canonical curriculum Markdown** (produced by the Curriculum Generator):
```
# منهج <subject>
# مقدمات أساسية            (optional block for sections printed before the first unit)
## <intro section title>
# الوحدة N: <title>          (unit group: directly followed by its chapters)
# الفصل N: <title>           (teaching block; a unit without chapters is itself the block)
## الدرس N: <title>          (lessons numbered 1..n per block; a block without lessons gets "الدرس 1: <block title>")
### <section> / #### <sub-section>
```
Books that print their works without unit/lesson labels (e.g. `2_high/روائع الأدب`, ten novels) keep the printed titles: `# <part title>` (or `# <subject>` when the book has no parts) and `## <work title>` with no invented "الدرس N". A block uses either labelled or unlabelled lessons, never both. The site's lesson map (`parseCurriculumOutline`) and `extractLessonBlocks` already handle unlabelled `##` titles.
`parseCurriculumOutline` (`curriculum_structure.ts`) turns this into `CurriculumUnit[]` (`groupTitle` = parent unit, `##` → lessons, `###` → subtopics, up to 20 per lesson). `extractLessonBlocks` treats every `##` heading as a lesson block, so intro sections are extractable too.

### 8.5 Fallback chain
Query intelligence fails → stop-word-filtered keywords, no scope (never treated as beyond-curriculum); embedding fails → lexical BM25 and graph only (never a different embedding model); rerank fails → fusion order; vector RPC fails → the other channels; corpus has no chunks → BM25 over the raw Markdown split by headings; VQA google fails → openai.

### 8.6 Curriculum Generator pipeline (`Curriculum Generator/src/lib`)
1. **Single-pass page extraction** (`extractPageWithDeepSeek`): each page is rendered in-process (`pdf_parser.ts`) and sent once, as image plus PDF text layer when readable, to DeepSeek 4.1. The reply is `PAGE_TYPE: content | toc | front_matter | exercises | answer_key | blank`, `---`, then the body: organized Markdown for `content`, outline lines (`الوحدة N: …` / `الفصل N: …` / `الدرس N: …` / `مقدمة: …`) for `toc`, empty otherwise. There is no separate TOC scan and no second request. Pages run in parallel (`batchSize`, default 8; about 36-50 pages/min measured on a scanned 272-page Arabic book). The prompt holds the Egyptian layout rules (lesson banners, compound numbers `1-3`, bullet topics merged into one title, badge numbers not fused into words, bare item labels never headings, margin headers/watermarks/links dropped, `#`/`##` reserved for unit/chapter/lesson openings and every other printed title kept as `###`). Worked material is kept (solved examples, نموذج تطبيقي, س/ج with answers); questions without answers, objectives boxes, timetables and front matter are skipped. `subjectRules` adds rules by subject name: religion (verses in ﴿ ﴾ with diacritics), Arabic (poetry hemistichs, tashkeel, grammar), foreign languages (no translation, vocabulary/grammar/reading kept), math and science (all math in LaTeX), social studies (dates, maps, timelines) and philosophy. Error handling: a page with neither image nor text fails instead of being stored as blank; empty `content` replies, API errors and timeouts are retried (`maxRetries`); replies cut at `max_tokens` 8192 are retried at 16384 and logged if still truncated; a missing header defaults to `content`.
2. **Outline** (`queue_processor.resolveOutline`, `deriveOutlineFromTocPages`): an operator outline (`outlineText`, entered at upload or in the preview modal's "فهرس الكتاب" editor, which re-exports instantly without API calls) wins. Otherwise pages classified `toc` within the first 15 pages (`TOC_SCAN_PAGES`) are combined and accepted only when they list 2+ units/chapters. Flat contents lists with 3+ top-level items and no units/chapters (a collection of stories or texts) are accepted too. Outline syntax: `قسم: title` is a part printed without a unit label, a bare title is a work printed without a lesson label (both keep their printed title, no invented number), author tags such as `لـ«…»` are stripped, unlabelled `الإجابات`/index lines are skipped, and leading unlabelled entries before the first labelled one become `مقدمة` sections. The extraction prompt now writes contents pages and work openings this way instead of inventing `الوحدة N`/`الدرس N` labels. As soon as it is accepted, the outline goes into the prompt of every later page, and it is stored on the checkpoint (`outline`, `outlineSource`, `tocPages`; each page keeps `pageType` and `tocText`). A `toc` page that is not accepted (usually a unit cover listing its lessons) keeps only its single unit/chapter heading. Checkpoints from the old two-step pipeline keep their stored outline.
3. **Assembly** (`buildCurriculumMarkdown` in `curriculum_cleaner.ts`): TOC and lesson-listing cover pages are dropped (`isTocPage`); for TOC-derived outlines `reconcileOutlineWithBody` merges bullet topics that the book's own lesson banner prints as one lesson and expands truncated titles, and `isOutlineIncomplete` falls back to heuristics when the body opens 2+ units the outline does not know; `alignMarkdownToOutline` maps headings to outline entries in order (explicit `#`/`##` headings win over same-titled `###` sections that appear earlier), emits missing parents, drops exact repeats (a heading that adds words to the active title, such as "التحليل الأدبي لرواية «الأرض»", is kept as a `###` section) and demotes everything else to `###`. Title words match up to one scanning error (`غبرات`/`عبرات`, `قليل`/`ليل`, `صخب`/`صح`). A cover page that prints only a title (often misspelled) opens the entry that is matched right after it. `markUnlabeledLessons` drops `الدرس N` labels that the model invented while reading the contents (opening headings rarely carry the outline's own numbers), and `wrapLooseLessons` groups top-level lessons into one `# <subject>` block. The trailing answer section (from the first `answer_key` page after which no new unit or lesson opens; answer headings that repeat earlier titles do not count) is dropped unless the outline lists the answers as an entry. Without an outline, `repairStructureHeuristically` keeps first declarations, drops continuation repeats and renumbers lessons sequentially. `stripClutter` removes objectives/outcomes boxes, back matter, publisher ads ("احرص على اقتناء") and emojis; `finalizeMarkdown` removes page markers and turns bare-label headings (`### ٢`, `### ب`) into plain text. Book-specific text replacements that corrupted other books (e.g. renaming any chapter containing "التفاعلات", rewriting "منطقة الحل") were removed.
4. **Validation:** `npm run verify -- <file.md>` (`scripts/verify_curriculum.mjs`) checks the canonical structure, increasing lesson numbers (gaps left by exercise-only lessons are warnings; blocks whose lessons are all unlabelled are accepted; mixing labelled and unlabelled lessons in one block is an error), math delimiters, leftover comments and emojis, and prints the lesson tree.
5. Output goes to `output/<stage_id>/<subject>.md`; stage ids are free text matching the admin catalog (suggestions in `KNOWN_STAGES`, `types.ts`). Rebuilt on 2026-09-28 and uploaded to production: `output/2_secondary_general/الفيزياء.md` (4 units, 6 chapters + intro, 16 lessons, boundaries checked against the book pages) and `…/الكيمياء.md` (3 chapters, 9 lessons). The physics outline is also saved as `Curriculum Generator/outlines/physics_2_secondary.txt`. Re-indexed on 2026-10-10 from its checkpoint (no API calls): `output/2_high/روائع الأدب.md` (intro "مصطلحات التحليل الأدبي لفن الرواية" plus the 10 unlabelled novels in book order, each opening on its own first page; outline in `outlines/rawai_aladab_2_secondary.txt`, stored as the book's manual outline). Also rebuilt `…/اللغة العربية (نحو).md` (4 units, 35 lessons with the book's numbers; exercise-only lessons 6 and 14 have no extracted content; outline `outlines/arabic_grammar_2_secondary.txt`, without the duplicated unit-cover tail and the answers entry) and restructured `…/اللغة العربية - النصوص.md` by hand (its checkpoint no longer exists): the reading-skills parts printed before the first unit (أولًا: مهارات الفهم، ثانيًا: مهارات التحليل، ثالثًا: مهارات المناقشة) now form the `# مقدمات أساسية` block instead of sitting unindexed above it, and publisher ads and running headers were removed; existing unit and lesson titles were kept. All three were uploaded to production on 2026-10-10 into their existing rows (`af7f7f13…` روائع الأدب, `f37ee6b1…` نحو - بلاغة, `b8803a6c…` اللغة العربية - النصوص) through the admin pipeline functions (`processCurriculumChunks`, `attachFileToCurriculum`, `saveCurriculumGraph`, full embedding, `curriculum_version` bump), replacing chunks, lesson maps (`units`) and graphs; no student had lesson progress on these subjects.

### 8.7 Voice Tutor pipeline (trial, `/voice`)
1. The student picks a subject, a speaking style (عامية مصرية / فصحى مبسطة) and a teacher voice (أستاذ / أستاذة), then starts the call. Both audio contexts are created inside the click so mobile browsers allow playback.
2. `/api/voice/session` mints the locked single-use token; the client lazy-loads `@google/genai` and calls `ai.live.connect` against `gemini-3.8-live` (v1alpha). The real `GEMINI_API_KEY` never reaches the browser, and the client cannot change the prompt or tools (verified: a client-side `systemInstruction` is ignored). `lockAdditionalFields` is deliberately omitted: Gemini rejects a field mask that includes `tools`, and omitting it locks every field.
3. **Silence check-ins.** When the teacher has finished and the student stays quiet (mic not muted, nothing playing, no reconnect), the client sends a hidden text note `[ملاحظة من النظام: الطالب ساكت بقاله N ثانية]` after 15 s, and once more 30 s after the teacher's reply; any student speech (mic level above 0.03 while no teacher audio plays, a transcription, or typing) resets the count. The prompt tells the model never to mention these notes: the first one gets room or a hint after a task, otherwise "إنت معايا؟ سامعني؟"; the later one gets one short "I'm waiting" sentence, then silence. Verified live 2026-10-04.
   Microphone audio (echo cancellation, noise suppression, auto gain) is downmixed by an AudioWorklet to 16 kHz PCM16 and streamed in 50 ms chunks (`sendRealtimeInput`). Model audio (24 kHz PCM) is scheduled gap-free on a Web Audio queue. Server-side voice activity detection gives native barge-in: when the student talks over the teacher the server sends `interrupted`, the client drops all queued audio and marks the cut caption. "وقّف الشرح" silences the rest of the current turn locally; a typed question (`sendRealtimeInput({text})`) also interrupts. Start-of-speech sensitivity stays at the default: the high setting made the model "hear" words in silence.
4. The prompt (`buildVoicePrompt`, English instructions, Arabic speech) sets the persona, dialect, spoken-only output (formulas said in words, no markdown/LaTeX/emojis), a real-teacher method (book excerpts are the source of truth, never read aloud: intuition first, exact textbook wording only where the exam needs it, then examples; detailed segments of usually 6-12 sentences), style adapted to the grade stage (primary, preparatory, secondary) and to the subject (math worked example then a similar problem by voice, physics from a phenomenon and prediction, chemistry, biology as a story, Arabic, languages with the student producing language, history and geography as cause-and-effect stories, religion, ICT), whole lessons shaped like a live class (hook, steps, key wording, worked example, student attempt, exam questions, wrap-up) without a fixed template, frequent rotating interaction moves (predict, solve a short problem now, explain back, Egyptian exam-style question, challenge, invite questions) with no bare "واضح؟", turns ending with a concrete task instead of permission questions like "تحب نجرب؟" (more explanation continues in the same turn), specific feedback followed by a fresh similar item, level raised or lowered by how the student does, live-call realism (unfinished speech or thinking sounds get silence or a tiny backchannel like "أيوه، كمل" instead of an explanation, background noise and other people ignored, tone of the student read to slow down or speed up), interruption etiquette, no praise for asking and no honorifics, and the scope rules of section 8.2.1. "The student is in charge": pace and style requests persist until changed ("كمل من غير ما تقف" switches to continuous mode with no checks, "كمل" resumes without recap, "أبطأ"/"اختصر", "عيد" re-explains differently, "اسألني" starts a quiz). **Language subjects** (English, French, German, Italian, Spanish via `voiceForeignLanguage` in `voice_policy.ts`) are explained in the chosen Arabic dialect while every foreign word or sentence is spoken in native pronunciation followed by its Arabic meaning; whole turns switch to the foreign language only on request, and translate / repeat slower / spell / pronounce / correct-my-sentence commands are carried out at once without a book search. The model is told that recognition can mishear speech into another script and must never reply outside Arabic (plus the taught language). Language subjects show their own starter chips (ترجملي جملة، صحّحلي نطقي، ...). **Speech recognition hints:** `voiceTranscription` locks `inputAudioTranscription` to `languageCodes: ['ar-EG', <subject language or en-US>]` (auto-detection sometimes transcribed Egyptian Arabic as Hindi) and `customVocabulary` of up to 40 textbook unit and lesson titles. Before any academic answer the model must call `search_curriculum` (one search per question, no near-duplicate searches); whole-lesson requests use `get_lesson` once and are taught fully in well-developed segments. The client runs tool calls through `/api/voice/tool`, shows them as "بحث في الكتاب" entries with source breadcrumbs, honours `toolCallCancellation`, and returns `sendToolResponse`.
5. Input and output transcriptions render as a live transcript; the orb shows state (listening cyan, speaking amber, searching violet) scaled by the audio level. The call opens with a hidden "بدأت المكالمة الآن." text (sent after `connect` resolves, since `setupComplete` arrives before it) so the teacher greets first.
6. Long calls: the client stores the latest `sessionResumptionUpdate` handle; on `goAway` or an unexpected close it requests a new token carrying the handle and reconnects (up to 3 times per call) with the conversation intact.
7. **Usage limits and fair use (8.7.1).** Tiers from `voiceTier` (`voice_policy.ts`): **free** 10 min per day, 10 min per call; **subscriber** (active paid plan) 60 min per day, 30 min per call; **unlimited** (admin or `unlimited_credit`) 600 min per day, 30 min per call. Days follow Cairo time. The feature never deducts points. A call reserves its seconds up front; the client sends a heartbeat every 30 s and an end report (`keepalive`, also on `pagehide` and unmount); ending settles the reservation to the real duration. A call that ends without a report is settled to its last heartbeat plus 30 s once its token has expired, so a crashed tab costs little and skipping heartbeats saves nothing. Starting a call while another is active settles the old one; its heartbeat then returns `active: false` and that client ends. Verified 2026-10-03 in local mode: reservation 600 s refunded to 3 s, resume grant 597 s, a stale call settled to 80 s, and a new call refused with 10 s left.
8. **Client experience (8.7.2).** The setup card states plainly that the feature is experimental, has a daily limit (10 free / 60 subscriber minutes), never deducts points and may stop at any time, and shows a remaining-minutes meter (free accounts get a link to the plans). During a call the head shows a countdown that turns amber in the last minute, plus a "باقي أقل من دقيقة" chip; at zero, or when the server closes the expired token, the call ends with a clear notice. The stage shows the teacher name and subject, a level-reactive orb, and a large live caption of the teacher's current sentence. Starter chips (اشرحلي الدرس الأول, اسألني سؤال على آخر درس, عندي مسألة مش فاهمها, لخصلي أهم النقط) appear until the student speaks. The full transcript is a toggle (open by default above 768 px). Controls are round call buttons: mic (also the M key), stop explanation, keyboard (typed question or equation), and a red end button. The screen is kept awake with the Wake Lock API, and `@google/genai` is preloaded when the page opens.
9. **Post-call feedback.** After a call of at least 30 s the setup card is replaced in place (no modal) by "إيه رأيك في المكالمة؟": 5 stars, then quick tags (positive for 4-5 stars, issues for 1-3), an optional suggestions box, "إرسال التقييم" and "مش دلوقتي". Sending shows a thank-you state. Sending or skipping pauses the prompt for 3 days (`egs_voice_feedback_at`).
10. **Key pool and seamless failover (8.7.3).** `gemini_keys.ts` reads `GEMINI_API_KEYS` (comma or whitespace separated) plus `GEMINI_API_KEY`. Each token is minted with the least recently used healthy key; failures cool a key (quota: 1, 2, 4, 8 then 15 min; invalid or revoked key: 6 h; other errors: 20 s), keys still cooling are tried last, and up to 4 keys are tried per request. Resumption handles only work on the project that issued them (verified: another key's token fails with "Requested entity was not found"), so the call's key slot is stored in `voice_usage.key_slot`: a reconnect resumes on the same key when it is healthy; otherwise (quota, invalid key, lost handle) the server mints a fresh session on another key and the client sends a hidden recap of the last 10 transcript lines so the teacher continues where it stopped without greeting again. The first connect is retried once on another key. Pool state is per server instance. Verified 2026-10-03: an invalid first key failed over in 0.7 s and was skipped afterwards; a quota drop moved the call without a new reservation. Google's API terms (section 2d) forbid circumventing usage limits, so the pool must only hold keys Google allows the project to combine (for example keys of the same billed project); free-tier keys from several accounts must not be pooled to multiply quota.
11. **Call logs.** Completed transcript entries (student and teacher lines with timestamps, interruptions, book searches with sources) are sent with every 30 s heartbeat and with the end report, and stored in `voice_calls`. The setup card discloses that the transcript is saved like written chats and used to improve the service according to the profile privacy setting. Admins see calls in the student's "سجل محادثات الذكاء الاصطناعي" viewer next to chat sessions (section 15), hidden like chats when the student opted out.

## 9. Coin (Points) Economy & Merit Leaderboard

### 9.1 Coins (Usage Currency & Renewal Integrity)
- **Fair Metering (`points_pricing.ts` + `metering.ts`).** Every paid AI call is charged from its real provider cost, never from client input:
  - Real cost = DeepSeek `deepseek-flash` list price per 1M tokens (peak: cache hit $0.006, cache miss $0.30, output $1.20; off-peak exactly half; peak = 01:00-04:00 and 06:00-10:00 UTC Monday-Friday) applied to the response `usage` (`prompt_cache_hit_tokens`, `prompt_cache_miss_tokens`, `completion_tokens`), converted at `USD_EGP_RATE` (default 55, accepted range 30-1000). Unmeasured helper calls use fixed estimates: `RAG_OVERHEAD_USAGE` per curriculum-grounded chat reply (3,000 cache-hit + 800 cache-miss input and 650 output tokens: DeepSeek query analysis with its cached lesson-map prefix, and the engagement check), `VISION_USAGE` per image, `TRANSCRIPT_REFINE_USAGE` per voice note (the DeepSeek transcript refinement). **Only DeepSeek usage is charged:** Alibaba Cloud embeddings, `qwen3-rerank` and Qwen3-ASR speech recognition run on the platform's free tier and are not billed to students (the per-second ASR charge was removed on 2026-09-30). When usage never arrives (client abort), a conservative estimate is charged (input chars / 4 as cache misses, output chars / 3).
  - **Point value** `POINT_VALUE_EGP` (about 0.0213 EGP of real cost per point) is derived from `KASHIER_PLANS`: `2 x min over plans of budget / (dailyCoins x (durationDays + 1))`, where `budget = price x (1 - 3.5% gateway fee - 10% worst-case margin) - 5 EGP overdraft reserve`. The rate is identical for every plan, so the 3-month plan always buys 1.5x the daily usage of the 1-month plan.
  - **Fair-use pace:** each active subscriber accumulates `sub_cost_egp` for the term. Before a charge, if it exceeds the plan budget released evenly over the term (plus a 2-day head start), that charge costs `FAIR_USE_MULTIPLIER` (2x) points. At the doubled rate no plan can exceed its budget even at 100% daily usage, so every plan stays profitable in every scenario (simulated worst case: 11.7% margin on `pro_3m`, 13.5% on `pro_2m`, 17% on `pro_1m` after fees; typical usage never reaches fair use). If `sub_cost_egp` is missing (migration not applied) subscribers are treated as over pace. Free accounts are never fair-use limited (their 5-point daily refill is the cap).
  - **Typical costs** (rate 55, off-peak / peak points): normal question 4.8 / 9.6, deep thinking 8.7 / 17.4, image 4.7 / 9.4 (two vision passes), voice minute 6.6 / 7.7, exam generation 8.9 / 17.9, exam grading 4.3 / 8.5, flashcards 5.8 / 11.6. Daily normal questions off-peak: `pro_1m` about 16, `pro_2m` about 18, `pro_3m` about 25.
  - **Atomic charging:** `db.chargeUsage` calls the `charge_usage(p_user, p_points, p_cost_egp)` RPC (SECURITY DEFINER, service_role only, rejects negative or oversized amounts), which in one UPDATE lowers `coins` (floor 0), moves any shortfall into `coin_debt`, and adds `p_cost_egp` to `sub_cost_egp`. Without the RPC it falls back to the legacy non-atomic `deductCoins` and logs an error.
  - **One AI request per account:** chat, exam generation and flashcard generation claim `acquire_ai_slot` (240 s lease, waits up to 8 s, else 429 `ai_busy`) and release it when done, so parallel requests cannot overdraw a balance. Image analysis, voice transcription and exam grading are charged atomically but not serialized.
  - **Overdraft:** a request is accepted while `coins > 0`; if the final reply costs more than the balance, the difference becomes `coin_debt` and is subtracted from the next daily refill (`coins = max(current, cap - debt)`). Activation (`activateSubscription`, `grantUserSubscription`) calls `resetMeteringTerm` to zero `sub_cost_egp` and `coin_debt`.
  - Nothing is charged when the AI provider cannot be reached; once the provider returns content it is charged even if the app later fails to parse it (prevents prompt-injected free generations).
- Floor at 0 with 2-decimal-place precision math (`toPreciseCoins`) preventing floating-point drift. `admin` role or `unlimited_credit` never deducted.
- Unregistered visitors: 0 coins. Trial coins (exactly 20.0 coins) are granted upon registration via `checkAndRecordTrialGrant` (ensuring no more and no less than 20.0 free points on account creation), with daily freemium renewal (5.0 coins) replenished at 12:00 AM Cairo Time. (The previous one-time 15.0-point compensation apology modal for legacy accounts registered on or before `2026-09-10T16:00:00Z` has been permanently retired and disabled from the user interface).
- **Registered users: daily replenishment and subscription expiry check (lazy, in `checkAndResetDailyCoins` via `getProfile`)**:
  - Automatically verifies `subscription_end_date`. If past expiration (`Date.now() >= subscription_end_date`), strictly downgrades `plan_type` to `'free'`, sets `subscription_status = 'expired'`, and stops all subsequent daily replenishments, preventing overstay.
  - Active subscribers are guaranteed daily renewal up to their plan cap (`DAILY_COIN_CAPS`: `pro_1m` 80.0, `pro_2m` 90.0, `pro_3m` 120.0, legacy `pro` 80.0, `max` 120.0) at 12:00 AM Cairo Time (`Africa/Cairo`) each calendar day throughout the active subscription. Free accounts receive an initial trial grant plus a sustainable daily freemium lifeline (`DAILY_COIN_CAPS.free = 5.0`) replenished each calendar day at 12:00 AM Cairo Time for fast queries, while Deep Thinking (Reasoning Mode), image analysis, and exam generation are strictly gated to active subscribers to maintain high upgrade intent without a permanent abandonment cliff.
  - Balances above the cap are never reduced.
  - **Schema Fallback & Resilience:** When optional subscription tracking columns (`subscription_status`, `subscription_start_date`, `subscription_end_date`, `subscription_plan_id`) are pending migration execution in Supabase, the backend (`db.ts`), verification engine (`currency_verifier.ts`), and client (`page.tsx`) automatically infer `'active'` subscription status and compute duration (30 days for `pro_1m`, 60 days for `pro_2m`, 90 days for `pro_3m`/`max`) from `plan_type` and `payment_transactions`. Self-healing logic in `checkAndResetDailyCoins` automatically detects and repairs truncated 30-day end dates on 2-month and 3-month accounts, backfilling accurate start/end dates so subscribers receive their complete academic term. The client formats the remaining duration into clear Arabic text (`3 أشهر (90 يوماً متبقياً)`, `شهران (60 يوماً متبقياً)`, `شهر واحد (30 يوماً متبقياً)`). Migration script `supabase_migration_fix_subscription_durations.sql` provides the database-level backfill for all existing accounts.
- Anti-tampering: Central server validation and HMAC-signed verification payloads guarantee accurate balances, prevent client-clock manipulation, and enforce strict expiration.
- 429 `limit_reached` from `/api/chat` when coins ≤ 0; 429 `ai_busy` when another AI request on the account is still running.
- All coin deductions happen server-side.

### 9.2 Ranking Points (`points` Merit System & Leaderboard)
- **Purpose:** A dedicated, non-depleting merit score tied directly to academic achievement and active participation (labeled `"نقاط الترتيب"` with a `Trophy` icon).
- **Currency Isolation:** Competition points (from exams and chat engagement) strictly increase ranking points (`points`) only; they never increase or award AI usage currency balance (`coins`). Usage currency is replenished exclusively via daily subscription renewals and package activations.
- **Exam First Attempt Scoring (`is_first_attempt = true`):**
  - **100% score:** 5 points
  - **>90% score:** 3 points
  - **>70% score:** 2 points
  - **>50% score:** 1 point
  - **≤50% score:** 0 points
  *(Points are awarded once on the student's first attempt per exam; subsequent retakes do not grant additional points).*
- **AI-Judged Chat Engagement:**
  - On chat completion (`/api/chat` done event), an asynchronous background classification evaluates the session's first meaningful user engagement (`classifyEngagement`).
  - If genuine engagement is detected in a new session (`engagement_points_awarded: false`), **+3 points** are awarded and synced to the profile and SSE done payload.
- **Leaderboard RPC (`get_leaderboard` & `get_user_leaderboard_rank`):** Ranks students by `points DESC`, with `study_streak DESC` and `avg_accuracy DESC` as secondary tiebreakers. Leaderboard results are strictly capped to the **Top 10** students. When an authenticated student is not in the Top 10, the server computes and returns their exact overall position (`user_rank`) along with points gap to the 10th rank. Test and trial accounts (non-student roles, or accounts with emails/names containing 'test', 'trial', 'اختباري', or 'تجريبي') are automatically filtered out from all leaderboard queries.

## 10. Authentication and Sessions

- **Password hashing:** PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 (WebCrypto, 100k iterations, 16-byte random salt, format `pbkdf2$iter$saltB64$hashB64`) in `auth_helpers.ts`. Legacy unsalted sha256 hashes are still verified and transparently re-hashed to PBKDF2 on successful login. Google accounts store `password_hash: ''`. The password-change flow calls `/api/auth/update-profile`.
- **Web session token:** `base64("userId:expiry:HMAC-SHA256(JWT_SECRET, 'userId:expiry')")`, 30-day expiry, constant-time comparison, no revocation/refresh; logout is client-side only. Secret from `JWT_SECRET` (prod refuses to run without it; dev fallback string exists).
- **Multi-Device Limit Security System (Max 3 Concurrent Devices):**
  - Accounts are strictly limited to a maximum of **3 active concurrent devices** (`user_devices` table).
  - Devices are identified by client UUIDs (`localStorage.getItem('egs_device_id')` on web) coupled with device metadata (browser fingerprint, IP address, user agent, Arabic OS/browser label).
  - **Auto-Revocation on 4th Device**: When a user registers or logs in on a new device while already having 3 active devices, the system automatically revokes all previous devices (`is_active = false`) and activates **only** the single new device (active count drops strictly to 1).
  - **Session Validation & Interception**: Protected endpoints validate the active device session via `validateUserSessionDevice`. Revoked devices receive a `401` response with `{error: 'device_session_revoked', code: 'device_session_revoked'}` and are immediately logged out with an informative Arabic explanation dialog.
  - **Device Self-Service Management**: The profile screen features an `"الأجهزة المتصلة والجلسات النشطة"` section with live active counts (`N / 3 أجهزة`), device list with device-type icons (`Smartphone`, `Tablet`, `Laptop`), last active timestamps, and remote single/all-other logout buttons.
  - **Admin Device Reset**: Administrators can view active device counts in the admin users table and reset a user's devices with a single click.
- **Deleted Account & Orphan Session Auto-Logout:**
  - When an account is deleted (via Admin Users, Admin Customer Service, or student self-service `/delete-account`), all database records including `user_devices` are immediately purged.
  - **Deterministic Initial Validation (`/api/config`)**: On browser startup or page load, if a token is provided in `localStorage` for a deleted or non-existent profile, `/api/config` explicitly returns `{ authenticated: false, user: null, user_not_found: true, session_invalid: true }`. The client immediately executes `handleForceLogout`, purging `egs_token`, `egs_user`, `egs_chat_sessions`, `egs_active_exam_id`, etc., and transitions to guest mode with an Arabic session-terminated dialog (`accountDeletedModal`).
  - **Live Heartbeat & Tab Focus Invalidation**: Active sessions automatically verify with `/api/config` whenever the tab regains visibility (`visibilitychange` with a 60-second cooldown to eliminate focus request storms and DevTools spam) and via a recurring 60-second background heartbeat. Deleted users are logged out immediately without needing to perform manual actions.
  - **Universal 401 Interception**: All protected API endpoints (`/api/chat`, `/api/chat/sessions`, `/api/chat/history`, `/api/exams/*`, `/api/flashcards/*`, `/api/auth/devices`, `/api/auth/update-*`, `/api/user/verify-currency`) verify that `db.getProfile(userId)` exists. If not, they return `401 Unauthorized` with `{ error: 'user_not_found', code: 'user_not_found' }`, which triggers `handleForceLogout` across the web client.
- **Simplified Registration Options & Egyptian Arabic Onboarding Flow:**
  - **Option 1 (Top — "تسجيل سريع"):** Google Quick Registration prominently displayed at the very top of the account creation modal. Clicking Google sign-in directly authenticates the student and creates their initial profile without blocking them with pre-login forms.
  - **Option 2 (Bottom):** Registration with **email and password only** (+ privacy/terms acceptance). No upfront stage, track, or name inputs are requested.
  - **OTP Verification (Email):** 6-digit crypto-random (`crypto.getRandomValues`), 10-minute expiry (`OTP_TTL_MS`); Resend email via `src/lib/email.ts` (register + password-reset templates). User enters 6-digit code with friendly Egyptian Arabic instructions ("وصلك كود على إيميلك!").
  - **Sequential Post-Registration Onboarding (Inside the Platform):** Upon completing signup via either Google or Email+OTP, the platform opens, and a neat sequential wizard guides the student:
    - **Screen 1 (Study path):** `StudyPathPicker` (`src/components/StudyPathPicker.tsx`) shows the active education systems as chips (hidden when only one system has active stages), the system's stages as cards, the stage's tracks as cards with descriptions, elective chips when the stage allows electives, and a live "موادك الدراسية" preview of the subjects the path unlocks (placeholders badged "قيد الإعداد"). Nothing is preselected; "next" stays disabled until `isPathComplete` (stage chosen, and a track when the stage has active tracks).
    - **Screen 2 (Name):** Student inputs or confirms their full name ("اسمك الكريم إيه؟"). Submitting screen 2 calls `/api/auth/update-profile` (`action: 'complete-onboarding'`) to atomically persist the stage, track, elective, and name.
    - **Automatic Starting Balance & Seamless Platform Entry:** Exactly 20.0 free points (no more, no less) are credited automatically to the student's wallet upon account creation (via email OTP or Google Quick Registration). Both welcome reward and free plan subscription flags are atomically marked as claimed (`welcome_reward_claimed = true`, `free_plan_claimed = true`), preventing redundant double-claims or point inflation during sequential onboarding completion (`complete-onboarding`). The previous blocking collection gateway screen (which required clicking two separate buttons before unlocking the platform entry button) was removed to prevent onboarding friction and failure cases. The introductory walkthrough (`startTour('chat')`) now launches immediately and seamlessly.
- **Google Sign-In:** web uses GSI button (`NEXT_PUBLIC_GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID`, hardcoded fallback ID in source). Prominently rendered at the top of both register and login views.
- **Guest identity:** web `egs_device_id` = `device_<random36>`. Client-supplied, spoofable.

## 21. Coding Guidelines

The mandatory rules live in **[CODING_GUIDELINES.md](CODING_GUIDELINES.md)** in this folder. Summary: no emojis in design; web responsive on all screen sizes with nothing missing; concise clean code with sparse comments and no emojis in code; latest stable technologies; zero critical vulnerabilities; when a change touches a feature linked to other pages/screens/layers, update those too so the feature works end-to-end — even when not explicitly asked (unless told not to); and after every completed task, update this PRD to reflect the change (rule 8).

Cross-cutting change map (what "linked" means in this project):
- **Chat protocol tags** (section 11): backend prompts (`tutor_prompts.ts`) + web parser (`page.tsx`).
- **API contract:** any route change → web fetch calls in `page.tsx`.
- **DB schema:** SQL migration + `db.ts` interfaces/queries + `db_data.json` local shape.
- **Theming:** web `globals.css` tokens.
- **Stages/tracks/subjects:** the Education Catalog (`education_catalog.ts`, stored in `system_settings.education_catalog`) is the only source; server validation (`validateStudentPath`, `sanitizeInitialPath`, catalog checks in admin routes), client lookups (`gradeName`, `StudyPathPicker`, `EducationCatalogManager`), AI prompt names (`registerStageCatalog` → `getGradeDisplayName`, `getStageLevel`) and the sponsor page (`grade_name` from `/api/payment/sponsor-info`) all read it. Never reintroduce hardcoded grade or track lists.
- **Curriculum Markdown format:** generator (`outline.ts`, `curriculum_cleaner.ts`), web chunker (`chunkMarkdownHierarchical`), structure parser (`parseCurriculumOutline`, `extractLessonBlocks`) and the validator (`scripts/verify_curriculum.mjs`) must agree on the canonical format of section 8.4.
- **SEO and feature naming:** `lib/seo.ts` `FEATURES` feeds route layouts, `/features` pages, `sitemap.ts`, root JSON-LD and `TAB_TITLES`. A new app route or feature needs an entry there, a layout using `featureRouteMetadata`, and the `pathMap` / `pathToTab` maps in `page.tsx`.
- **Chat media format:** `chat_media.ts` (`IMAGE_RE`, `VOICE_RE`, `media:` prefix), `scripts/offload_chat_media.mjs`, `chat/route.ts` `parseMessage`, and the `page.tsx` renderers (`mediaSrc`) must agree on `[IMAGE_MESSAGE:...]` / `[VOICE_MESSAGE:...]`.
- **Legal pages:** `/terms` reads plans from `KASHIER_PLANS` and voice limits from `VOICE_LIMITS`; a change to points, trial grants, refund rules, AI providers or data use must update the `/terms` and `/privacy` text in the same change.
- **Plans or point pricing:** `plans.ts` (catalog), `points_pricing.ts` (provider prices, margins, peak windows), `db.ts` `DAILY_COIN_CAPS` + `normalizePlanId`, hard-coded plan cards in `page.tsx` and `/sponsor`, the Terms page, and this PRD. Re-run the worst-case check whenever prices, plans or margins change.
